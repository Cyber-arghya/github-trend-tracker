## 🤖 AI/ML Highlights

*   **scenario-labs/skills:** A powerful framework for equipping AI agents with structured "skills" to interact with digital content creation pipelines. This is crucial for developing sophisticated, goal-oriented agents capable of generating and manipulating various media types, from concept to production-ready assets.
*   **NVIDIA/auto-ontology:** Addresses a fundamental challenge in enterprise AI: grounding agents with accurate, contextualized data. By creating a semantic layer and mapping business terms to data sources, it enables agents to query databases reliably, reducing hallucinations and improving the trustworthiness of AI-driven insights. This is a significant step towards robust enterprise-grade AI.
*   **FareedKhan-dev/kimi-k3-in-c:** An astonishing feat of engineering demonstrating extreme optimization for LLM inference. Running a multi-trillion-parameter model on a single CPU with minimal RAM (8GB) in pure C99 is remarkable. While the inference speed (seconds per token) might limit general interactive use, it opens doors for highly constrained edge devices, embedded systems, or specialized batch processing where resource efficiency is paramount. It's a proof-of-concept for future LLM accessibility.
*   **Thysrael/Horizon:** An application demonstrating the practical use of large language models (Claude, GPT) for content processing, specifically news aggregation and summarization. It showcases a common pattern for leveraging generative AI to distill information and deliver personalized content experiences. This highlights the growing trend of AI as a consumer-facing utility.
